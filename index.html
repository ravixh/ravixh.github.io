ALBUM · 2026 · RAVIR'S WIRE

SANGAM

Emotional hip-hop · Cinematic · Lyrical storytelling

Sangam means a confluence — a union. The album brings together Ravir Scott's experiences, struggles and creative vision into one cohesive musical narrative: a cinematic emotional journey through silence, waiting and inner conflict, blending emotional rap with ambient sound and storytelling. Every track is written, composed and produced by Ravir himself.

“SANGAM is not just an album — it is a reflection of who I am and what I've survived. It connects my past, my pain, and my purpose into one sound.” — Ravir Scott, on the album's release

The flute work

Flutes of Sanātana

Bansuri · Devotional · Instrumental

Alongside the rap records, Ravir's recent work turns to the bamboo flute. Flutes of Sanātana is a bansuri-led release rooted in the timeless — sanātana, “the eternal” — where the breath of the flute carries the melody instead of words.

It's the other half of the SANGAM idea: hip-hop tells the story out loud; the flute says what the story feels like.

About

Ravir Scott (born Ravish Kumar) is an Indian independent music artist, author and developer from Sonma, Begusarai, Bihar. With no financial backing or production house behind him, he taught himself music composition, filmmaking and technology — and built a fully independent career on determination and practice.

His sound blends emotional rap and hip-hop with cinematic production and, increasingly, the bansuri flute. His releases are distributed worldwide through RouteNote and SpaceMedia under his own label, Ravir's Wire, and indexed across MusicBrainz, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music and IMDb.

Beyond music, he writes books, builds AI products, and runs a simple creed: one person, with focus and dedication, can produce professional work without a big studio.

Lone Yet Loyal
